Craft工艺

Built the slow way, on purpose.刻意慢下来的制作方式。

01 / 03

Solid wood, honestly built实心木材，诚实结构

No veneers, no particleboard. Every panel is solid timber, joined the way furniture was joined before shortcuts existed.不用饰面板，不用刨花板。每一块板材都是实木，采用最传统扎实的接合工艺。

02 / 03

Small workshops, not factories小型工坊，而非流水线

Our partner workshops produce in limited runs, with the same makers seeing a piece from rough timber to finished form.合作工坊坚持小批量生产，同一位工匠会全程参与从原木到成品的每一步。

03 / 03

Made to be inherited为传承而生

Repairable joints, replaceable parts, finishes that age well. Furniture meant to outlive the room it started in.可维修的接合方式，可更换的部件，经得起岁月的表面处理。这些家具的寿命，远超它们最初所在的房间。
