Who We Are我们是谁

A factory-minded team with a design eye.既懂制造，也懂设计。

LW Home Furniture focuses on furniture-style bathroom vanities for international B2B customers. We combine product design, manufacturing discipline and export execution so a concept can move from reference image or drawing to repeatable production.LW Home Furniture 专注于面向国际 B2B 客户的家具化浴室柜。我们把产品设计、制造管理与出口执行结合起来，让一张参考图或一份图纸能够真正落地为稳定量产。

01 / PRODUCT

We start with how the product should live in a real bathroom.先从真实浴室里的使用方式出发。

Proportion, storage, sink configuration, hardware, material and finish are considered together — not added one by one after the cabinet is drawn.比例、收纳、盆型、五金、材料和表面效果会一起考虑，而不是画完柜体后再逐项补上。

02 / PRODUCTION

Manufacturing decisions are made early.制造逻辑从开发初期就介入。

Structure, machining, finishing, assembly and packaging are reviewed before mass production so the finished piece is both attractive and repeatable.结构、加工、涂装、组装与包装在量产前就会共同评估，确保产品既好看，也能稳定重复生产。

The Team团队

One product. Four teams. One delivery standard.一件产品，四个团队，同一个交付标准。

We do not present anonymous departments. Each function has a clear job in moving a product from idea to shipment and keeping repeat orders consistent.我们不把团队做成模糊的部门介绍。每个职能都有明确任务，共同推动产品从概念走向出货，并保证返单的一致性。

Inside the Factory走进工厂

Where design becomes a repeatable product.让设计变成可稳定复制的产品。

Our production system covers machining, shaping, assembly, sanding, finishing, hardware installation, inspection and export packing. The goal is not simply to make one good sample — it is to make the next order match it.我们的生产体系覆盖加工、造型、组装、打磨、涂装、五金安装、检验与出口包装。目标不只是做出一个好样品，而是让下一批订单依然保持一致。

From Brief to Container从需求到整柜

A clear process for custom and repeat business.为定制与返单建立清晰流程。

For importers, distributors, retailers and project buyers, visibility matters. Each stage has a clear output so product decisions are confirmed before they become production problems.对进口商、分销商、零售商与工程采购来说，流程透明非常重要。每个阶段都有明确输出，尽量在量产前解决产品决策问题。

Quality System质量体系

Quality is a process, not a final inspection.质量不是最后一道检查，而是一套过程。
